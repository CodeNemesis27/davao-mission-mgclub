About the club

Trained to lead. Called to serve.

A Master Guide is a leader who has completed the advanced leadership training of Pathfinder ministry. In the Davao Mission, our Master Guides guide clubs in local churches, train new staff and give young people a safe place to grow in faith, character and skill.

  • Lead and train

    We run staff training and leadership courses so every club in the Mission has prepared, dependable leaders.

  • Mentor the youth

    Pathfinders and Adventurers learn by walking beside someone who has done it before. We make that walk happen.

  • Serve the community

    Feeding programs, clean-up drives and disaster response, organized with churches and neighbors across Davao.

  • Camp and teach outdoors

    Camporees, honors and field skills, from knots and first aid to navigation by map and compass.

Lead.Serve.Pass it on.
